Privacy policy

Buster OS · Last updated October 13, 2026

Buster OS is a private app used by one household. Its only users are the two adults who live there, and it is not offered to anyone else.

What Google data it accesses

With our own permission, Buster OS reads our own Google Calendars using read-only access: event titles, times and locations. It cannot create, change or delete calendar events, and it does not access any other Google data.

How the data is used

  • To show us our own schedule.
  • To write a short daily summary for us. The summary is written by an AI assistant (Anthropic's Claude), so the day's calendar details are sent to it for that purpose only.
  • To send that summary as a notification to our own phones.

Where it is stored

Calendar data is kept on our own home computer. It is not stored anywhere else by us, and it is deleted whenever we choose.

What we never do

  • We never sell, rent or trade Google user data.
  • We never use it for advertising.
  • We never share it with anyone outside our household, other than the AI assistant described above, which writes the summary.

Revoking access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
