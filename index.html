Buster OS

Buster OS is a private app used by one household. It is not a product, it has no public sign-up, and no one outside the household can use it.

It helps the two of us keep track of our own schedule, to-dos and home in one place. With our permission, it reads our own Google Calendars (read-only) to show our schedule and to write a short private daily summary for us.
